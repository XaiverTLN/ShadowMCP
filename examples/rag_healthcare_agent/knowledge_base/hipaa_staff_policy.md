# Staff Handoff and Minimum Necessary PHI Policy

## Nurse shift handoff

The departing nurse gives the incoming nurse a concise, private handoff covering current condition, active orders, allergies, pending tests, and immediate follow-up needs. Staff should use approved clinical systems and verify the recipient's identity before sharing patient information. Handoffs should take place away from public areas and be documented when required by department procedure.

## Minimum necessary disclosure

Staff may access or disclose only the protected health information needed for their assigned work. Confirm the recipient and purpose before sending records, use approved channels, and avoid copying unrelated patient details into messages or reports. Report suspected misdirection or inappropriate access through the clinic's privacy process.

## Encryption and device handling

Use clinic-approved encrypted systems for stored or transmitted PHI. Do not place PHI in personal email, consumer file-sharing services, or unapproved removable media. Lock unattended workstations, protect authentication factors, and report lost devices or suspected credential exposure promptly.
